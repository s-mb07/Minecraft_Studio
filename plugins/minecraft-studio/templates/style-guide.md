## Banned patterns (generic filler)
- "Embark on a journey", "Unleash", "Dive into", "Delve", "Tapestry", "Epic adventure awaits"
- Starting every quest with "Now that you have..."
- Exclamation marks in more than 1 quest out of 10
- Explaining what the task box already says
